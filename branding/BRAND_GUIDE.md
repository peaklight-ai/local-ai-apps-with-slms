# Baynetna - Brand Guide

## Brand Name

**Baynetna** (بيناتنا)

## Etymology

Baynetna is a Lebanese Arabic word meaning **"between us"** - representing privacy, confidentiality, and trust.

## Brand Concept

Baynetna embodies the essence of private, confidential communication and document processing. The name reflects our core promise: what happens in Baynetna, stays in Baynetna.

### Key Messaging
- **Privacy First**: "Between us" - your documents, your secrets, completely private
- **Local Processing**: Everything stays on your device, nothing leaves your machine
- **Trust & Security**: Built on the Lebanese cultural value of keeping things "baynetna" (confidential)

## Brand Values

1. **Privacy** - Your data never leaves your device
2. **Trust** - Built with transparency and open-source principles
3. **Security** - Local AI processing means no cloud, no leaks
4. **Simplicity** - Easy to use, powerful results

## Tagline

**"powered by peaklight.ai"**

## Brand Story

In Lebanese culture, when something is "baynetna," it's sacred - a pact of confidentiality between trusted individuals. Baynetna brings this cultural value to AI-powered document processing, ensuring your sensitive information remains truly private.

Unlike cloud-based AI tools that send your data to remote servers, Baynetna processes everything locally on your device using Small Language Models (SLMs). Your documents, summaries, and conversations stay baynetna - between you and your computer.
